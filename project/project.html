Youth Welfare Claims and Unemployment in the UK

Are young people claiming benefits more than other age groups? If so, is it because they can't find work, or because they aren't expected to look for it? And where is it worst?

Aim

The UK government has been debating whether to spend more on welfare benefits, in particular for young people due to the high unemployment rate among them. Some argue that young people on benefits are not looking for work, while others argue that they are not getting enough support to find work. This project aims to explore the reasons behind the high unemployment rate among young people and whether they are receiving enough support to find work, and where that help falls short.

The argument for spending more depends on which gap matters most: young claimants getting no work-coach support, support that is mostly compliance rather than help, or too few entry-level jobs in their area.
